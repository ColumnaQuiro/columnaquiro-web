## What determines the price of a chiropractic visit?

The cost of seeing a chiropractor depends on several factors: the length and type of initial assessment, how complex the case is, the clinic's location, and whether several follow-up sessions are needed. There's no single price that applies to every clinic or every patient.

## First visit vs. follow-up visits

The first consultation is usually more thorough, since it includes a health history, physical examination, and often a personalized treatment plan, so it typically has a different cost than later visits, which tend to be shorter and focused on the adjustment or follow-up care.

## Treatment plans and session packages

Many clinics, including ColumnaQuiro, offer packages or multi-session plans that can be more cost-effective than paying for each visit separately, especially when the treatment plan requires ongoing care over time.

## How to find out the exact price for your case

The most reliable way to know the real cost is to contact the clinic directly or book a first visit, where your specific case will be assessed and you'll be informed of the available options.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the first day more expensive?**
It usually has a different cost because it includes a more thorough assessment, but this varies by clinic.

**Are session packages cheaper?**
In many cases, yes, though it's best to check directly with the clinic for current options.

**Does the price depend on my specific problem?**
The complexity of the case and the number of recommended sessions can play a role, but this is defined after the initial assessment.
