At ColumnaQuiro in Valencia, **your first visit costs €70**. It includes a full assessment (history, examination and mobility tests), your first adjustment, and a plan with the number of sessions we estimate for your case. It takes about an hour. You pay nothing when you book: you pay the €70 at the clinic on the day.

In Spain a chiropractic session usually costs **between €30 and €60**, with the first visit a little more because it includes the assessment. Our first visit sits at the top of that range because it lasts an hour, includes the first adjustment and always takes place in a private room just for you.

## What each visit includes

| Visit | What it includes | Length | Price at ColumnaQuiro |
|---|---|---|---|
| First visit | History, examination, mobility tests, first adjustment and care plan | About 60 minutes | €70 |
| Follow-up visits | Progress check, adjustment and exercises for home | 15 to 20 minutes | Explained at your first visit, together with session packs |

Follow-up visits cost less than the first because they don't repeat the assessment. If your plan includes several sessions, we offer packs that work out cheaper than paying for each visit. We give you the exact price in writing before you start, and you don't commit to anything on day one.

## What your total cost depends on

The price per session is only part of it. What changes the total most is **how many sessions you need**, and that depends on:

- **How long you've had the pain.** Pain of a few weeks usually improves faster than pain you've had for years.
- **What's causing it.** A stiff neck from poor posture is not the same as sciatica or a herniated disc.
- **What you do between sessions.** Home exercises and posture advice reduce the number of visits.

Many patients notice an improvement after the first or second session. If you're not improving as expected after a few sessions, we tell you and change the plan or refer you to another professional, rather than adding more visits.

## Does health insurance cover it?

Spain's public health system doesn't cover chiropractic, and most private insurers don't either, though some policies include it or refund part of the cost. We explain it in [Does Spanish health insurance cover chiropractic?](/en/blog/does-spanish-health-insurance-cover-chiropractic/). If your insurer refunds it, we give you an invoice to claim with.

## How to tell if a price is fair

A low price isn't worth it if the person treating you isn't properly trained. Chiropractic isn't regulated in Spain, so check that your chiropractor has full university-level training and is a member of the Spanish Chiropractic Association (AEQ). [How to choose a chiropractor in Spain](/en/blog/how-to-choose-a-chiropractor-in-spain/) has a checklist.

Be wary, too, of long fixed plans you have to pay for up front before you know whether you're improving.
