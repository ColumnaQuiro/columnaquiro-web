## What is aggressive multiple sclerosis?

Multiple sclerosis (MS) is an autoimmune disease that affects the central nervous system. In some people the disease progresses with more frequent or severe relapses and a faster build-up of disability; this pattern is often referred to as "aggressive MS." It is a serious condition that always requires ongoing care from a specialized neurologist.

## Important: chiropractic care does not cure MS

We want to be very clear here: chiropractic care does not cure or replace neurological treatment for multiple sclerosis. Any decision about medication, disease-modifying therapies, or relapse management should always be made with your neurology team.

## What role can chiropractic care play?

As a complement, and always coordinated with your neurologist, gentle and adapted chiropractic care may help ease muscle tension related to reduced mobility, improve postural comfort for people with walking or balance difficulties, and support an overall sense of nervous system wellbeing.

At ColumnaQuiro we adapt every technique to a person's abilities and limitations, always working with extra gentleness and staying in communication with the rest of the medical team. The goal is never to "cure" the disease, but to support the person's day-to-day comfort.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can chiropractic care cure or slow multiple sclerosis?**
No. There is no evidence that chiropractic care cures or changes the course of the disease. Treatment must always be directed by neurology.

**Is it safe to receive chiropractic adjustments if I have MS?**
It can be, in many cases, using gentle and adapted techniques — but it's essential to check with your neurologist first.

**What benefits can chiropractic support offer?**
It may help with postural comfort, muscle tension, and general wellbeing, but never as a substitute for medical treatment.

**Should I mention it if I'm having an active relapse?**
Yes, it's essential to report any relapse or change in symptoms before any session.
