## Searching for a "chiropractor near me" in the Eixample

The Eixample is one of the liveliest districts in central Valencia, and one that generates a lot of local searches whenever someone needs support for their back or neck. If you live or work in this area, ColumnaQuiro is a short walk away, at Carrer dels Vivons, 29.

## Neighbourhoods we serve

Although our centre is in the Eixample, many of our patients walk or take public transport from nearby areas:

- **Russafa**, right next door, with the Russafa Market as a landmark.
- **Ciutat Vella**, a few minutes from the historic centre.
- **Extramurs**, just across Gran Vía.
- **Camins al Grau**, for those working near the business district.
- **Pla del Real**, next to the Turia Gardens.

If you're not sure whether we're within reach, you can check the exact location on [our chiropractic centre page in Valencia](/en/about-us/chiropractic-valencia).

## The kind of care we offer

At ColumnaQuiro we support each person with an individualised plan, not a one-size-fits-all approach. Some of the most common situations we see at the centre include:

- Discomfort related to [sciatica](/en/symptoms/sciatica) or [low back pain](/en/symptoms/low-back-pain).
- [Neck pain](/en/symptoms/neck-pain) and stiffness from long hours of screen or office work.
- Ongoing support for [scoliosis](/en/symptoms/scoliosis).
- Care during pregnancy and through a baby's or child's early years.
- Preparation and recovery for athletes, including those training for the [Valencia Marathon](/en/blog/chiropractic-preparation-for-the-valencia-marathon-how-to-avoid-injuries).

Before suggesting any adjustment, we always carry out a full assessment of your case, because not every discomfort responds the same way to chiropractic care.
