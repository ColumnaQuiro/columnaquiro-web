## A chiropractic centre in the heart of Russafa

If you live, work, or are just staying in Russafa and you're looking for a chiropractor nearby, ColumnaQuiro is at Carrer dels Vivons, 29, right on the border between Russafa and the Eixample district of Valencia. We chose this neighbourhood because it's a meeting point for so many people in the city centre: local residents, office workers, and runners training along the Turia Gardens.

## What you'll find when you arrive

The centre has private, closed rooms, so you'll be alone with your chiropractor for the whole session — no rush, no shared spaces. Before any adjustment, we carry out a full initial assessment: your health history, a physical examination, and, if needed, a review of any imaging tests you already have.

## How we care for your spine at every session

At ColumnaQuiro we support each patient with a care plan adapted to their spine and nervous system, using precise [chiropractic adjustments](/en/blog/how-much-does-a-chiropractor-cost) and complementary techniques depending on the case. The goal isn't a promise of a cure, but helping your body move and function with the best possible mobility and balance.

## Who we care for in Russafa and nearby

We welcome very different profiles from the neighbourhood and the city centre: people who spend long hours at a desk, pregnant patients, families with babies and children, and runners preparing for events like the [Valencia Marathon](/en/blog/chiropractic-preparation-for-the-valencia-marathon-how-to-avoid-injuries). If you'd like a general idea of who this discipline can support, you can read more in [our guide on who chiropractic is for](/en/who-is-chiropractic-for).

## How to find us

We're just a few minutes' walk from the Russafa Market and well connected to the rest of the city. You can check the exact address and opening hours on [our centre page](/en/about-us/chiropractic-valencia) before booking your appointment.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is ColumnaQuiro in Russafa or in the Eixample?**
The centre sits right on the border of both neighbourhoods, at Carrer dels Vivons 29 — a short walk from both the Russafa Market and the Eixample.

**Do I need a medical referral to book an appointment?**
No, you can book your first visit directly. During the initial assessment we review your history and, if we find your case needs another specialist, we'll let you know.

**How long does the first visit take?**
It's usually longer than follow-up sessions, since it includes a full health history and physical examination before proposing any care plan.

**Do you see children and pregnant patients at the Russafa centre?**
Yes, our team includes chiropractors specialised in maternal and paediatric care.

**How do I book my appointment?**
You can book online through [our appointment page](/en/book-appointment).
