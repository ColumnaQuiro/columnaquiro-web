## What is the Webster Technique?

The Webster Technique is a specific chiropractic protocol developed for the care of pregnant women, focused on analyzing and aligning the pelvis, along with the muscles and ligaments that support it. Its goal is to optimize the mother's pelvic function throughout pregnancy.

## How is it applied during pregnancy?

The chiropractor evaluates the position of the pelvis and checks for imbalances in the surrounding muscles and ligaments, particularly the round ligament. From there, gentle, specific adjustments are applied, fully adapted to each stage of pregnancy, aiming to reduce tension and improve pelvic balance.

## Benefits for the mother during pregnancy and birth

A more balanced pelvis can support better function throughout pregnancy and birth, helping reduce discomfort associated with pelvic strain and allowing the body to move more freely in the final weeks of pregnancy. Many mothers report feeling more overall comfort after their sessions.

## Frequently asked questions

**At what point in pregnancy can the Webster Technique be started?**
It can be applied at different stages of pregnancy, always adapting the technique to the stage of gestation and each patient's condition.

**Is it safe for the mother and baby?**
Yes, it involves gentle, specific adjustments with no pressure on the abdomen, designed specifically for pregnancy.

**Does the Webster Technique guarantee a complication-free birth?**
It doesn't guarantee a specific outcome; its goal is to optimize pelvic function and the mother's comfort as part of comprehensive pregnancy care.

**Should I combine it with my OB-GYN's care?**
Yes, the Webster Technique is complementary care and should always go hand in hand with your regular pregnancy follow-up.
