## What does a chiropractor actually do?

A chiropractor specializes in diagnosing and caring for disorders of the musculoskeletal system, particularly the spine, and how these affect the nervous system. The work starts with a full postural and functional assessment to identify movement restrictions, muscle imbalances, or compensations that may be causing pain or limiting movement.

## Main techniques used

The spinal adjustment is the best-known technique: a precise, controlled manipulation aimed at restoring normal movement to a joint. Chiropractors also typically combine soft-tissue work (myofascial release, assisted stretching), postural assessment, and exercise recommendations to round out treatment.

## Benefits of chiropractic care

Common benefits include reduced back and neck pain, improved joint mobility, better posture, and less accumulated muscle tension. Many patients also notice improved sleep quality and an easier time going through daily activities without discomfort.

## Who is it a good fit for?

Chiropractic care can help people dealing with back or neck pain, tension headaches, posture-related discomfort from office work, athletes looking to optimize mobility, and pregnant women seeking care adapted to their stage of pregnancy. The technique is always tailored to each person's characteristics and needs.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does a chiropractic adjustment hurt?**
It shouldn't. There may be a brief sensation of pressure, but the goal is relief, not pain.

**How many sessions will I need?**
It depends on the case. Some people notice improvement after just a few sessions, while others benefit from a longer care plan.

**Does chiropractic care replace medical treatment?**
No. It's complementary care that works alongside other healthcare professionals, not a replacement for necessary medical treatment.

**Is it safe for all ages?**
Yes, there are techniques adapted for children, adults, pregnant women, and older adults, always adjusting the force and approach to each case.
