## What is coxalgia?

Coxalgia is the medical term for hip pain. It can affect one or both sides, and its origin varies depending on age, activity level, and the presence of other conditions like osteoarthritis.

## Symptoms

Pain can appear when walking, climbing stairs, or sitting for long periods, usually accompanied by stiffness and reduced mobility that gets in the way of daily activities.

## Most common causes

The most frequent causes include joint wear (osteoarthritis), inflammation of tendons or bursae, previous injuries, and, in some cases, systemic conditions affecting several joints at once.

## The role of chiropractic care

Using manual techniques focused on the relationship between the spine, pelvis, and hip, chiropractic care can help improve joint mobility and reduce the muscle tension associated with hip pain.

## Frequently asked questions

**When should I see someone about hip pain?**
If the pain lasts more than a few days, limits your mobility, or appears suddenly for no clear reason.

**Is it always osteoarthritis?**
No, hip pain can have many different causes; a professional should evaluate it first.

**How long does it last?**
It depends on the cause, but with the right care plan many people improve within a few weeks.

**Can I keep exercising?**
Generally yes, adjusting the intensity and avoiding movements that make the pain worse.
