## What is a cervical disc protrusion?

The intervertebral discs act as cushions between the neck vertebrae. A protrusion happens when a disc bulges slightly out of its normal position without rupturing, which can sometimes irritate nearby nerve structures.

## Common symptoms

It can cause neck pain that radiates into the shoulder or arm, tingling or a numb feeling in the fingers, and in some cases stiffness that limits head movement.

## Causes and risk factors

Natural wear with age, prolonged screen-related postures, repetitive neck-flexion movements, and sometimes a previous injury can all contribute to a cervical disc protrusion.

## How chiropractic care can help

After an assessment confirms the case is suitable for conservative care, gentle cervical spine adjustments, combined with mobility and strengthening exercises, help reduce pressure on nearby nerve structures and gradually ease symptoms.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is a disc protrusion the same as a herniated disc?**
Not exactly. A protrusion is an earlier stage, where the disc bulges without rupturing, unlike a herniated disc.

**Is an MRI needed for diagnosis?**
In many cases a clinical assessment is enough to guide diagnosis, with MRI reserved for persistent symptoms or warning signs.

**Can I keep working at a computer?**
Yes, by adjusting your workstation ergonomics and taking frequent breaks to change position.

**When is urgent medical evaluation needed?**
If significant weakness develops in the arm or hand, or symptoms worsen quickly, see a doctor without delay.
