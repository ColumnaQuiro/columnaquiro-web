## What is scoliosis, and why does it show up in the teen years?

Scoliosis is a sideways curvature of the spine that often becomes more noticeable during the rapid growth of adolescence. In many cases there's no single identifiable cause (idiopathic scoliosis), but this growth phase is when changes tend to appear quickly enough to be spotted.

## Signs parents should keep an eye on

Watch for one shoulder sitting higher than the other, an uneven hip line, a rib hump that appears when bending forward, or clothes that no longer hang evenly on the body. None of these signs confirms a diagnosis on its own, but any of them is a good reason to schedule an evaluation.

## The importance of medical follow-up

Whenever scoliosis is suspected, it's essential that a doctor or spine specialist assess the degree of curvature, usually with an X-ray, and determine whether ongoing monitoring, a brace, or another treatment is needed. Chiropractic care does not replace this medical follow-up.

## How chiropractic care can help

As a complement to medical monitoring, chiropractic adjustments adapted for teenagers can help improve spinal mobility, ease muscle tension related to the curvature, and support better day-to-day postural awareness.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can chiropractic care correct scoliosis?**
It doesn't correct or cure it; its role is supportive, helping with comfort and mobility while the medical specialist leads the main treatment plan.

**At what age is it usually detected?**
Most often between ages 10 and 15, coinciding with the peak growth spurt.

**Is scoliosis painful?**
Not always. Many teenagers report no pain at all, which is why periodic postural screening matters.

**When should I take my child to the doctor?**
If you notice any of the body asymmetry signs mentioned above, it's best to seek an evaluation as soon as possible.
