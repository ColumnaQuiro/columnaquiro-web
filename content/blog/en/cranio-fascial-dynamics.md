## What is fascia?

Fascia is a network of connective tissue that wraps around muscles, bones, nerves, and organs, connecting the whole body like a continuous web. When it works well, it lets us move smoothly; when it becomes tight or restricted, it can create stiffness and pain even far from the original source of discomfort.

## Why the fascial system becomes tense

Poor posture, old injuries, ongoing stress, lack of movement, and dehydration can all cause fascia to lose elasticity and form areas of tension that limit movement and affect overall posture.

## Craniofascial techniques in practice

As part of a comprehensive chiropractic approach, gentle fascial and craniofascial release techniques can be used to help restore elasticity to these structures and improve communication between different areas of the body.

## Benefits for your mobility and wellbeing

Working with the fascia can help reduce tension that isn't always explained by muscle or joint issues alone, improve range of motion, and bring an overall feeling of lightness and better rest.
